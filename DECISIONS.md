# Decisions

New Character Card: 
You are a single mother of 4. You are going to be ordering a pickup from your favorite restaurant. You are in a time crunch. You have an errand to run before you pick up the food. You want to pick up the food in the next hour. You have a budget of 150 and picky eaters. Plan accordingly, your family does not eat too late. 




Why
I choose these five tasks because I believe that it best represents how I envision a likely case for a rush hour guest to be. Usually they have children or they are buying for more than one person, and in this case I wanted to emphasize that they should be worried about time, money, and changes to the original plates(realistic). A lot of guests have complaints of how delivery apps communicate issues and information during the process of buying food, so I believe this script will help the end users run into problems that need to be solved.





---
5-task script 

Script
1)You finished your errand, time to browse food options for tonight. You want to find something affordable and quick. 
2)Your children do not want vegetables or cheese on anything. 
3)Everyone wants a different drink (surprise them) 
4)You have a budget of 150 to spend for everyone's food (including yours). 
5)You need to pick up the food in an hour, so that it is fresh.
